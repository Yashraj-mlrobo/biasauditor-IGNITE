Real Bias. Real Consequences.

Unchecked ML models perpetuate historical inequalities. The COMPAS algorithm famously misclassified defendants based on race, while Adult Income prediction models consistently undervalue female earners.

IGNITE provides the transparency needed to audit these systems, using rigorous mathematical definitions of fairness to ensure equitable outcomes.
